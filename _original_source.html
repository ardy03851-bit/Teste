Click+Collect

Save time with our great-value collection slots. We’ll pick and pack your weekly shop with freshness in mind, ready for you to swing by and collect.

  • Get great value

    We'll pick and pack your groceries and safely store them for you to collect. The minimum order value is only €30, and there's no additional Click+Collect fee, so it's perfect for smaller shops.

  • Ready when you are

    There's no need to wait at home if you can't. With lots of collection locations, and many more opening soon, there's plenty of convenient slots to choose from. Just pick a time that works for you.

How does Click+Collect work?

  • 1. Book a slot

  • 2. Do your weekly shop – there’s a minimum spend of just €30

  • 3. Make changes to your order if you need to

  • 4. Collect your groceries during your booked slot time

Forgotten something?
You can make changes to your order up until 11:45pm the night before your delivery or collection slot

How to collect

We have a few different ways to collect, depending on which location you choose. When you arrive at the car park, please follow the Click+Collect signs, which will guide you to our collection points.

  • Collecting from our vans

    For some locations, you'll be greeted by a colleague from one of our Click+Collect vans who will bring your shopping to you

  • Using a QR code

    If the name of your collection location ends with 'mobile phone required', when you come to collect, you'll see instructions on a sign asking you to use your mobile phone to scan a QR code to let us know you've arrived. We'll then bring your shopping out to you.

Click+Collect with help from our colleagues
If the name of your collection location has no extra information, a colleague will be at the collection point, ready to help you

Home delivery

Get your shopping done in no time and enjoy home delivery slots that fit around your schedule. Need a last-minute change? You can add to your order up until 11:45 the night before your slot.

Why choose home delivery?

Shop from the comfort of your home, and we'll deliver straight to your doorstep at a time that suits you. If you need a hand, our delivery drivers are there to help.

  • 4. We'll deliver your shopping to your doorstep
